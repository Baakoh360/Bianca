How to work it out

  1. Write down what each service costs, including tip and travel.
  2. For one-off buys (like clip-ins), divide the price by the months you expect to use them.
  3. Multiply price × times per month. A fill every 3 weeks is about 1.4 times a month.
  4. Add it all up, then multiply by 12 for the yearly cost.
