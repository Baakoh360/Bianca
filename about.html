About

Beauty education you can trust.

The Beauty Journal exists because beauty advice is either generic, sponsored or contradictory. We think you deserve better.

Our promise

  • Unfiltered: we tell you what things really cost, what can go wrong and what to avoid.
  • Evidence-led: routines are based on manufacturer and professional guidance, not trends.
  • Transparent: if we ever feature a partner brand or discount code, we'll say so clearly.
  • Practical: every guide is built so you can actually do it, with video and checklists.

Who it's for

Women aged 18–35 — students, young professionals, creators and beauty enthusiasts — who want salon-quality results at a price and effort that make sense.
