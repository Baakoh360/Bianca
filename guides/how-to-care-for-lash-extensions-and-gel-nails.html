Lash + Nails

How to care for lash extensions and gel nails

Lash extensions and gel nails fail for the same few reasons: moisture, oils, picking and pulling. The good news is that a short routine covers both.

By The Beauty Journal editors · Updated 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

On this page

  1. Lash extension care
  2. Gel nail care
  3. The simple weekly routine
  4. FAQ

Lash extension care

Cleanse gently, regularly

Debris, oils and makeup break down the adhesive. Clean your lashes every few days with a foaming lash cleanser, brush through with a clean spoolie, rinse and pat dry. Don't rub.

Avoid oils and harsh products

  • Skip oil-based makeup removers and cleansers around the eyes.
  • Don't use waterproof mascara, or any mascara unless your technician says so.
  • Keep heavy steam and very hot water away from your face in the first couple of days.

Hands off

Picking, rubbing and pulling can take your natural lash with the extension. Sleep on your back where you can, and brush lashes only with a clean spoolie.

Tip: book infills every two to three weeks. Waiting longer leaves heavy, grown-out extensions that put more stress on natural lashes.

Gel nail care

Hydrate the cuticles

Massage cuticle oil in daily. It keeps the skin and nail flexible, which reduces lifting and cracking.

Protect from water and chemicals

Wear gloves when washing up or using cleaning products. Long soaks weaken the bond.

Don't use your nails as tools

Opening cans, scraping labels and prising things apart are the fastest way to chip or snap a set.

Remove safely

  1. Gently file through the top coat.
  2. Soak cotton pads in acetone, place on each nail and wrap in foil for around 10 to 15 minutes.
  3. Slide the softened gel off with a wooden stick. If it resists, re-wrap rather than scraping.
  4. Buff lightly, wash your hands and apply cuticle oil and hand cream.

Frequently asked questions

How soon can I get lash extensions wet?

Follow your technician's instructions. Many advise keeping them dry for the first 24 to 48 hours while the adhesive fully sets.

Can I use mascara on lash extensions?

Most technicians advise against it. It can clog the lashes and is hard to remove without pulling. A lash serum should only be used if your technician says it's safe for your set.

Why do my gel nails keep lifting?

Common causes are oils or moisture on the nail during application, too much water exposure, and using nails as tools. Cuticle oil and gloves for cleaning help.

Is it OK to peel off gel polish?

No. Peeling takes layers of your natural nail with it. Soak off properly or have it removed by a professional.

This guide is educational and not medical advice. Always patch test new products, follow your technician's instructions and seek professional help if you have pain, swelling or an allergic reaction.
