How to remove gel nails at home safely

Peeling gel off takes layers of your natural nail with it. A patient soak-off protects your nails and takes about 20 minutes.

By The Beauty Journal editors · Updated 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

On this page

  1. What you need
  2. Step by step
  3. Afterwards
  4. FAQ

What you need

  • A coarse nail file or buffer
  • Pure acetone
  • Cotton pads cut into pieces, and foil squares
  • A wooden or orange stick
  • Cuticle oil and hand cream
  • Petroleum jelly to protect the skin

Step by step

  1. Rub petroleum jelly around the skin of each nail, avoiding the nail itself.
  2. Gently file through the shiny top coat until it looks dull. Don't file down into the natural nail.
  3. Soak a piece of cotton pad in acetone, place it on the nail and wrap with foil.
  4. Wait around 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Unwrap one nail and gently push the softened gel off with the stick. If it doesn't slide off, re-wrap and wait another few minutes. Don't scrape or force it.
  6. Wash your hands, lightly buff any leftover residue and apply cuticle oil and hand cream.
Tip: work in a well-ventilated room, away from flames, and keep acetone off furniture and plastic.

Afterwards

Nails can feel dry or a little thin afterwards. Use cuticle oil daily and give them a break if needed. See how to rebuild weak nails.

Stop and seek advice if you have pain, burning or an allergic reaction.

Frequently asked questions

Can I just peel off my gel nails?

It's best not to. Peeling removes layers of the natural nail and can leave it thin and weak.

Can I use non-acetone remover?

Gel polish usually needs acetone to break down. Non-acetone remover is unlikely to work on most gels.

Does this work on builder gel or hard gel?

Hard gels and builder gels may not soak off. These are usually filed down. Ask the technician who applied them.

This guide is educational and not medical advice. Always patch test new products, follow your technician's instructions and seek professional help if you have pain, swelling or an allergic reaction.
