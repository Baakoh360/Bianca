Costs

What beauty upkeep really costs per month

Nobody tells you the monthly total until you've already booked. Here is an honest, indicative breakdown so you can decide what's worth it.

By The Beauty Journal editors · Updated 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

On this page

  1. The monthly numbers
  2. Three realistic routines
  3. Where to save without cutting corners
  4. FAQ

These are indicative UK price ranges that vary by region, salon and technician. Check current prices locally.

Three realistic routines

The minimalist

Clip-ins, home gel nails, natural lashes. Roughly £30–£80 a month once your kit is bought.

The balanced

Clip-ins, salon gel nails and occasional lash infills. Roughly £100–£220 a month.

The full set

Tape-ins, lash infills and salon nails. Roughly £135–£300+ a month.

Where to save without cutting corners

  • Extend the time between appointments safely: good aftercare means fewer repairs.
  • Buy once, care well: a better clip-in set lasts longer than two cheap ones.
  • Learn removal: safe at-home removal saves a salon fee each time. See our gel care guide.
  • Check the real total: ask what's included, such as removal, infill pricing and any deposit.

Frequently asked questions

What's the cheapest way to keep up all three?

Clip-in hair extensions are a one-off cost, and a home gel kit replaces salon visits for nails. Lashes are the hardest to do yourself, so many people alternate with a natural-lash routine.

Is it cheaper to do things at home?

Often, after the starting kit. But a poor application can cost more in repairs, so learn the technique first.

This guide is educational and not medical advice. Always patch test new products, follow your technician's instructions and seek professional help if you have pain, swelling or an allergic reaction.
